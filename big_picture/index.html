01 · The challenge

Too many frameworks. Too many indicators.

SMEs face CSRD, the EU taxonomy, GRI, the SDGs and ISO standards, all at once. WP1 collected 2,710 raw sustainability indicators from the literature and partner practice.

For one SME, that is not guidance. It is noise.

02 · From noise to structure

Five pillars bring order.

Every indicator is sorted into one of five operational pillars. Here each light is one real indicator from the WP1 catalogue.

    03 · One factory at a time

    The model lands in each SME.

    The framework only matters where it is used: on the shop floor. Here, a pilot factory near Metz. The Green Factory Model works in three layers: vision, pillars, action.

    04 · Layer 1 · Strategic vision

    Start with an ambition.

    An initial diagnostic, a 5-year ambition and a clear management commitment, aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals.

    05 · Layer 2 · Operational pillars

    Five pillars, one digital thread.

    Each building is a pillar. Digital tools are not a silo: they connect and measure all five.

    Click a building or a pillar to see its indicators.

    06 · Layer 3 · From model to action

    A generic model, tailored to each SME.

    1. Company profileSector, size, maturity, resources
    2. KPI selectionFiltered by relevance and feasibility
    3. BaselineQuantitative diagnostic of today
    4. SMART targetsSpecific, measurable objectives
    5. Action planAn operational roadmap

    07 · Measure progress

    The Performance Wheel.

    One score per pillar, one overall score and a maturity level from 1 to 5. At a glance, an SME sees where it stands and where to act next.

    Illustrative scores, to be calibrated with pilot SME data.
